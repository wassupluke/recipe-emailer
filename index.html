Caesar Wrap

1 serving | Love and Lemons

Ingredients

  • 6 small romaine lettuce leaves
  • 1 curly kale leaf, stemmed
  • 2 radicchio leaves
  • ⅓ cup cooked chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • ½ avocado
  • ¼ cup sliced red onion
  • Parmesan shavings, optional
  • Lemon wedge, for squeezing
  • Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • Homemade Caesar Dressing or Vegan Caesar Dressing, for drizzling
  • 1 12-inch flour tortilla

Instructions

Place the romaine, kale, radicchio, chickpeas, avocado, and red onion on a cutting board. Top with shaved Parmesan, if using, and squeeze with lemon juice. Season with sprinkles of salt and pepper. Drizzle generously with the dressing and roughly chop everything on the cutting board, mixing it together as you chop. Load the salad mixture into the tortilla and fold to close. Serve with more dressing on the side.

Chicken and Sweet Potatoes

4 servings | Spend With Pennies

Ingredients

  • 4 medium boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 6-7 ounces each)
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil (divided)
  • 1 tablespoon Cajun seasoning
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes (cut into ¾-inch cubes)
  • 1 red onion (cut into ½-inch wedges)
  • 2 tablespoons honey (or hot honey)
  • 4 cloves garlic (sliced)
  • ¾ teaspoon salt (more to taste)
  • ¼ cup pecans (optional)

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed baking pan with parchment if desired. Pound the chicken breast to an even thickness. In a large bowl, toss the chicken with 1 tablespoon of olive oil and Cajun seasoning. Arrange in the center of the baking sheet. In the same bowl, toss sweet potatoes, onion, garlic, remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil, honey, and salt. Arrange around the chicken. Roast for 15 minutes. Stir the vegetables and add the pecans if using. Roast for an additional 10 to 15 minutes or until the chicken reaches 165°F and the sweet potatoes are tender. Transfer the chicken to a cutting board and let rest for 5 minutes. While the chicken is resting, stir the vegetables into any juices on the pan. Thinly slice the chicken and serve.

Shrimp and Broccoli

3 servings | Well Plated

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons canola oil (or neutral cooking oil of choice, divided)
  • 1 pound large or jumbo raw shrimp (peeled and deveined)
  • ⅛ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ⅛ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 broccoli crowns (chopped into florets, about 4 to 5 cups)
  • 3 tablespoons water
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic (about 3 cloves)
  • 1 ½ tablespoons minced fresh ginger (about a 1 ½ inch piece)
  • 4 green onions (chopped with white/light green and dark green parts divided)
  • Prepared rice (for serving)
  • Red pepper flakes (optional)
  • ⅓ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • ¼ cup water
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons sesame oil
  • 2 teaspoons cornstarch

Instructions

Mix In a small bowl or large liquid measuring cup, whisk together the sauce ingredients: soy sauce, water, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and cornstarch. Set near the stove. Cook In a large skillet or wok, heat the 1 1/2 tablespoons of the canola oil over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp to the skillet in a single layer. Season with the salt and pepper. Cook 1 to 2 minutes per side, until just pink and curling into a "C" (shrimp overcooks fast!). Remove to a plate. Sear Add the remaining 1 1/2 tablespoons canola oil, then add the broccoli in a single layer. Let it cook undisturbed for 2 minutes. Steam Stir the broccoli, pour in the water, then cover. Let steam for 2 minutes to soften. Add Stir in the garlic, ginger, and white/light green parts of the onions. Toss and cook another 1 to 2 minutes, until the broccoli is crisp-tender. Toss Pour in the sauce (restir it a bit if it has separated) and add the shrimp. Toss to combine and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, until the sauce is glossy, thickens a bit, and everything is coated. Garnish Sprinkle with the dark green onion parts. Serve immediately over rice with red pepper flakes if you'd like a little heat.
