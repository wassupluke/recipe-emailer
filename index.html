Pork Tenderloin Recipe

4 servings | Spend With Pennies

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pork tenderloin (* 1 tenderloin)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 12 ounces sliced mushrooms (cremini or white)
  • ½ medium yellow onion (diced)
  • ¼ teaspoon dried thyme leaves
  • ½ cup white wine (or chicken broth)
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tablespoon butter (softened)
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley (optional garnish)

Instructions

Pork Tenderloin Preheat oven to 400° F. Line a baking sheet with foil. Season the pork with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the tenderloin in the pan, rotating until all sides are golden. Place on prepared baking sheet. Cook for 18 to 20 minutes or until a thermometer reads an internal temperature of 145℉. Allow to rest for at least 5 minutes before slicing. Optional Mushroom Sauce Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and starting to soften. Add onion and thyme. Reduce heat to medium and cook for 5 minutes more. Add the wine and Worcestershire to the pan. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring to loosen any bits off the pan, until the liquid has nearly evaporated from the pan. Add the butter to the pan, stirring to melt. Sprinkle in the flour and stir to coat the mushrooms. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes more. Gradually whisk in broth and bring to a boil. Simmer until thickened, about 4 to 5 minutes. Taste and season with salt and pepper as desired. Serve with sliced tenderloin.

Main: Miso Soup

4 servings | Love and Lemons

Ingredients

  • 1 (3-inch) piece kombu
  • 4 cups water
  • 3 tablespoons wakame dried seaweed
  • ¼ cup white miso paste
  • ⅓ cup chopped green onion
  • 6 ounces silken tofu, cubed
  • Tamari, to taste

Instructions

Gently rinse the kombu piece. Place it in a medium pot with the water and gently simmer for 10 minutes. Don’t let it boil, or the kombu flavor will turn bitter. Soak the wakame in a small bowl of warm water for at least 5 minutes to rehydrate. Remove the kombu from the soup. In a small bowl, stir the miso paste together with some of the hot broth until the mixture is smooth, then stir it back into the soup. Drain the wakame and add it to the soup pot along with the green onions and tofu. Simmer over very low heat for 1 to 2 minutes. Season to taste with tamari and serve.

Side: Brown Butter Mashed Potatoes with Crispy Garlic and Sage

8 servings | Half Baked Harvest

Ingredients

  • 4 pounds Yukon Gold or russest potatoes (peeled and cubed)
  • 8-12 big cloves garlic (thinly sliced)
  • 8 tablespoons cold salted butter
  • 1/3 cup fresh sage leaves
  • 6 ounces cream cheese (at room temperature)
  • 1 tablespoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1-2 cups whole milk or heavy cream
  • sea salt and pepper

Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9x13-inch baking dish. In a large pot, cover the potatoes and 2 garlic cloves with water. Season generously with salt and boil until fork-tender, about 15–20 minutes. Drain well, then return to the hot pot to let any excess moisture evaporate. Mash the potatoes with the cream cheese, onion powder, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Gradually add 1/2–1 cup milk or cream until creamy. Don't overmix! Meanwhile, melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and sage. Cook for 3–5 minutes, reducing the heat as the butter foams, until the garlic is lightly golden and the sage is crispy. Remove the crispy garlic and set aside. Reserve half the brown butter for serving. Chop the sage and stir into the potatoes with the remaining brown butter. Spoon into the prepared baking dish, make little swirls on top, and pour over 1/2 cup milk or cream. Don't stir! Cover with foil. Bake for 25 minutes, or until warmed through. Spoon the reserved brown butter overtop and finish with the crispy garlic. Serve warm! To Make Ahead Oven: Prepare through step 5. Cover tightly and refrigerate for up to 2 days. Bake, covered, at 350°F for 35–45 minutes, or until heated through to 165°F in the center, allowing additional time if needed. Gently stir in warm milk or cream if needed. Finish with the reserved brown butter, crispy garlic, and sage. Slow Cooker: Prepare the potatoes and brown butter as directed through step 4, then mix the potatoes as directed in step 5. Transfer to a buttered slow cooker and keep warm for up to 4 hours, maintaining a temperature of at least 140°F. Stir occasionally, adding warm milk or cream as needed. Finish with the reserved brown butter, crispy garlic, and sage just before serving.

Spicy Salmon Sushi Bowls

4 servings | Gimme Some Oven

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked short-grain sushi rice
  • 1 ¼ cups water
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon fine sea salt
  • 1 pound fresh salmon filets
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise
  • 1-2 tablespoons sriracha (to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon rice vinegar (or lime juice)
  • ½ teaspoon sugar
  • 2 Persian cucumbers (halved lengthwise and chopped (or smashed))
  • 2 green onions (thinly sliced)
  • 1-2 teaspoons soy sauce
  • Optional toppings:
  • 1 avocado (sliced)
  • furikake seasoning
  • chili crisp
  • pickled ginger

Instructions

Cook the rice. Rinse the rice under cold water until it runs mostly clear. Add to a saucepan with the water and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let sit (covered) for 10 minutes. Season the rice. Stir together the rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Gently fold into the warm rice. Let it cool slightly while you prep the rest. Prep the salmon. Cut the salmon into 1-inch cubes and transfer to a bowl. Toss with soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic powder, and black pepper until evenly coated. Air fry. Arrange the salmon in a single layer in the air fryer basket. Cook at 400°F for 6–8 minutes, shaking halfway through, until lightly crisp on the edges and just cooked through. Make the spicy mayo. In a small bowl, whisk together the mayo, sriracha, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sugar until smooth. Taste and adjust the heat level as needed. Toss the veggies. In a small bowl, toss the cucumbers and green onions with 1–2 teaspoons soy sauce until lightly coated. Assemble the bowls. Divide the rice between bowls. Top with warm salmon, soy-tossed cucumber mixture, and avocado. Finish and serve. Drizzle generously with spicy mayo and sprinkle with furikake. Add any extra toppings you love (chili crisp, pickled ginger, extra sriracha, etc). Serve immediately with soy sauce and enjoy!
